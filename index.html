About ATL Dart Club

Nerf As A Service, for real.

ATL Dart Club (ADC) is Atlanta's premier Nerf entertainment company — NAAS: Nerf As A Service. ADC got its start as an award-winning youth STEM program with the City of Atlanta, and grew into a high-energy, highly-engaging Nerf battle experience — a fresh alternative to paintball or laser tag, minus the bruises and the price tag.

Since then, ADC has been featured in local publications and recognized by organizations like Boys & Girls Clubs of Metro Atlanta and Emory University's Goizueta Business School for its unique, innovative experience.

We don't have a single home venue — we bring the fun to you. Parks, gyms, office parking lots, church lawns, backyards: if there's room to run, then we can have foam flinging fun.

Client experience, enjoyment, and safety are our top priorities, on every booking, every time.
