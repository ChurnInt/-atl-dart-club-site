About ATL Dart Club

Nerf As A Service, for real.

ATL Dart Club (ADC) is Atlanta's premier Nerf entertainment company — NAAS: Nerf As A Service. We deliver a fun, highly-engaging Nerf battle experience as a high-energy alternative to paintball or laser tag, minus the bruises and the price tag.

We don't have a single home venue — we bring the battlefield to you. Parks, gyms, office parking lots, church lawns, backyards: if there's room to run, we can turn it into a war zone for an afternoon.

Client experience, enjoyment, and safety are our top priorities, on every booking, every time.
