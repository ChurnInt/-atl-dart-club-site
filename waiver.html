Georgia activity waiver & photo release.

This agreement is given under the laws of the State of Georgia. Completing the form is required to participate in any ATL Dart Club event.

1. Released parties

This waiver is given to ATL Dart Club, a company of ChurnInteractive LLC, and to their owners, officers, members, employees, contractors, volunteers, hosts, sponsors, and any venue or property owner where an event is held (together, the “Released Parties”).

2. Who we are and how we run events

ATL Dart Club is a family-friendly Nerf entertainment company. We prioritize fun, safe play, and an inclusive environment. Our crew hosts the experience, supplies gear, sets game rules, and referees play. We will do all that we reasonably can to keep the event organized and welcoming. That commitment does not mean the activity is free of risk.

3. The activity

I understand that ATL Dart Club events are physical, active Nerf battles and related games. They may include running, jumping, crouching, sudden direction changes, foam darts, blasters, barriers, outdoor or indoor surfaces, weather, other participants, and hosted game modes. Events may take place at parks, gyms, offices, churches, backyards, or other sites chosen by the client or ADC.

4. Assumption of risk

I know this is active recreation. The potential for injury is real. Risks include, without limitation: being struck by foam darts or equipment; trips, slips, and falls; collisions with people, barriers, or the ground; sprains, strains, bruises, cuts, dental injury, eye injury, broken bones, heat-related illness, allergic reactions, and in rare cases more serious injury or death. I voluntarily assume all risks of participating, whether those risks are known or unknown, and whether they arise from my own actions, the actions of other participants, the condition of the site, or the ordinary operation of the event.

5. Release of liability (Georgia)

In return for being allowed to participate, I hereby release, waive, and forever discharge the Released Parties from any and all claims, demands, and causes of action for personal injury, property damage, or other loss arising out of or related to ATL Dart Club events, including claims based on the ordinary negligence of the Released Parties, to the fullest extent permitted by the laws of the State of Georgia.

I agree that ATL Dart Club, a company of ChurnInteractive LLC, will not be held liable for injuries or losses connected to this activity, except to the extent Georgia law does not allow a release (including, where applicable, gross negligence, willful misconduct, or wanton conduct). I intend this release to be as broad and inclusive as Georgia law allows. If any part is held unenforceable, the rest remains in effect.

6. Indemnification

I agree to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Released Parties from any claim, loss, or expense (including reasonable attorneys’ fees) arising from my participation, a listed minor’s participation, or my breach of this agreement, to the fullest extent permitted by Georgia law.

7. Health, medical care, and emergency contact

I confirm that I (and each listed minor) am/are physically able to take part in active play. I will tell ADC staff about any condition, allergy, or limitation that could affect safe participation. If I am injured, I authorize the Released Parties to obtain emergency medical care. I understand I am responsible for medical costs. ADC does not provide personal health insurance.

8. Rules, gear, and family-friendly conduct

I will follow all ADC safety rules, referee instructions, and equipment guidelines, including eye protection when required. I will not aim at faces at close range, alter gear, or bring outside blasters unless ADC staff approve them. ADC may remove anyone who creates an unsafe or unwelcome environment. We are a family-friendly company; abusive, harassing, or unsafe conduct is not allowed.

9. Photo, video, and likeness release

ADC photographs and records events. I grant ATL Dart Club and ChurnInteractive LLC a perpetual, royalty-free, worldwide right to use my name, voice, image, and likeness — and those of each listed minor — in photos, video, and audio from events, in any media, for marketing, social, website, press, and promotional purposes. I waive any right to inspect or approve finished uses and any claim for compensation or for invasion of privacy related to those uses.

10. Adults and participants under 18

Only a parent or legal guardian who is 18 or older may sign for a person under 18. By listing a minor below, I represent that I have legal authority to do so. To the fullest extent Georgia law allows, I sign this waiver on that minor’s behalf, I release the Released Parties from claims I could bring as parent or guardian, and I agree to indemnify the Released Parties against claims brought by or on behalf of that minor.

11. Electronic signature and governing law

I agree that typing my legal name below is my electronic signature under the Georgia Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (O.C.G.A. § 10-12-1 et seq.) and has the same effect as a handwritten signature. This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Georgia, without regard to conflict-of-law rules. I consent to exclusive venue in the state courts of DeKalb County, Georgia. This is the entire agreement on these subjects. It binds my heirs, family, and legal representatives.
